# AI-Assisted Engineering

## Specification Metadata

**Specification ID:** STD-0011

**Domain:** Standards

**Version:** 1.0

**Status:** Draft

---

# Purpose

This Standard defines how Artificial Intelligence participates in
engineering activities performed under AAOE.

AI augments engineering work by assisting humans throughout the
engineering lifecycle while operating under human governance.

---

# Standard

AI systems may participate in engineering activities throughout the
software lifecycle.

AI participation shall operate within the governance established by AAOE
Standards.

Human accountability remains unchanged regardless of the degree of AI
assistance.

---

# Principles

AI-Assisted Engineering shall:

- augment human engineering
- improve engineering productivity
- improve engineering quality
- preserve engineering governance
- preserve traceability
- remain transparent
- remain reviewable
- remain technology independent

---

# AI Capabilities

AI systems may assist with:

- Requirements Analysis
- Specification Drafting
- Architecture Proposals
- UX Design Support
- Design System Assistance
- Code Generation
- Code Review
- Testing
- Documentation
- Refactoring
- Migration
- Knowledge Retrieval
- Implementation Reporting

Participation does not imply autonomous approval.

---

# Human Responsibilities

Humans remain responsible for:

- Engineering Governance
- Engineering Decisions
- Architecture Approval
- Risk Acceptance
- Compliance
- Final Approval
- Release Approval

Human accountability shall not be delegated.

---

# Engineering Workflow

```text
Engineering Intent
        ↓
Human + AI Collaboration
        ↓
Engineering Artifact
        ↓
Human Review
        ↓
Architecture Review
        ↓
Approval
```

AI participation may occur at multiple stages of the workflow.

---

# Transparency

Engineering artifacts should identify when AI materially contributed to
their creation.

AI-generated work shall remain reviewable by humans.

Engineering decisions shall remain explainable.

---

# Traceability

AI-assisted engineering should remain traceable through:

- Specifications
- Work Orders
- Implementation Reports
- Architecture Reviews
- ADRs

AI participation shall not reduce engineering traceability.

---

# Exceptions

Organizations may restrict AI participation in regulated or sensitive
engineering activities.

Any restrictions should be explicitly documented.

---

# Relationship to Human-Governed Engineering

This Standard operationalizes
[STD-0008 — Human-Governed Engineering](0008-human-governed-engineering.md).

AI operates within governance established by humans.

---

# Relationship to Architecture Reviews

[Architecture Reviews](0009-architecture-reviews.md) remain responsible
for evaluating engineering artifacts regardless of whether they were
created by humans, AI, or through collaboration.

The origin of an artifact shall not affect governance requirements.

---

# Relationship to Implementation Reports

[Implementation Reports](0010-implementation-reports.md) should document
significant AI participation when relevant to engineering traceability.

---

# Compliance

Engineering work conforms when:

- AI participation remains governed.
- Human accountability is preserved.
- Engineering artifacts remain reviewable.
- Traceability is maintained.
- Architecture Reviews remain mandatory.

---
