# Scope

## Definition

Scope is the precise statement of what is, and is not, included in a Work Order. It is one of the canonical Work Order sections, distinct from Constraints (which bound how the objective may be achieved) and Objective (which states the outcome itself).

## Why It Matters

One Work Order should accomplish one coherent engineering objective. Keeping scope small and explicit reduces ambiguity, simplifies review, improves AI collaboration, and increases traceability — an oversized or vague scope is one of the most common ways a Work Order becomes hard to execute or review.
