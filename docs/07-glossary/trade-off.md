# Trade-off

## Definition

**Note:** "Trade-off" is used consistently across AAOE Guides and Standards (Architecture Review, ADRs, Human–AI Collaboration) but does not yet have a single canonical definition as a standalone term. This entry is a candidate for future refinement.

Provisionally: a trade-off is a consequence accepted in exchange for a benefit when an architectural or engineering decision is made — recorded, where significant, as part of an Architecture Decision Record's consequences.

## Why It Matters

Surfacing trade-offs explicitly, rather than leaving them implicit, is one of the things Architecture Review exists to do. An ADR that records consequences without naming the trade-off behind them leaves future readers unable to tell whether the trade-off still holds.
