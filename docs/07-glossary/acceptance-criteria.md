# Acceptance Criteria

## Definition

Acceptance Criteria define how it will be known that a piece of engineering work is done, and done correctly. They are a required part of a Specification, alongside objectives, constraints, assumptions, and expected behavior.

## Why It Matters

A specification without acceptance criteria leaves "done" as a matter of opinion. Acceptance Criteria give implementers — human or AI — a concrete, checkable target, and they give reviewers a basis for evaluating whether implementation actually satisfies the specification it was built from.
