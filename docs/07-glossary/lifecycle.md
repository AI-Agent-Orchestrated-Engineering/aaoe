# Lifecycle

## Definition

**Note:** "Lifecycle" is reused as a pattern name across several independently defined AAOE specifications — the Engineering Lifecycle, the Discovery Lifecycle, the Specification Lifecycle, the Knowledge Lifecycle, and the Architectural Decision Lifecycle — but does not have a single standalone definition applicable to all of them. This entry is a candidate for future refinement.

Provisionally: a Lifecycle is the ordered sequence of governed states a specific kind of artifact or knowledge progresses through, from its origin to its retirement or supersession.

## Why It Matters

Each AAOE Lifecycle defines states specific to what it governs — a Specification's lifecycle (Draft, Accepted, Revised, Superseded, Archived) is not the same shape as the Engineering Lifecycle (Architecture through Next Work Order). Readers should not assume one lifecycle's states apply to another.
