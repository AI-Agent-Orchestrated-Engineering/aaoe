# Specification Lifecycle

## Definition

The Specification Lifecycle is the governed progression a Specification moves through: Draft, Accepted, Revised, Superseded, and Archived. It governs how Specifications are created, evolved, versioned, superseded, and retired while preserving traceability to the Discoveries and Architectural Decisions that justified them.

## Why It Matters

Specifications are living artifacts, not one-time documents. Without a defined lifecycle, their evolution would be untraceable and their history could be silently overwritten. The Specification Lifecycle ensures specifications evolve incrementally, preserve history, and remain traceable even as they change.
