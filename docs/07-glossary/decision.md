# Decision

## Definition

**Note:** Standalone "Decision" is used loosely throughout AAOE documentation. The canonical, formally defined concept is the **Architectural Decision** (see the Architectural Decision Lifecycle), which is distinct from its record, the Architecture Decision Record. This entry is a candidate for future refinement to clarify when "Decision" is used generically versus as shorthand for Architectural Decision.

Provisionally: a Decision is the act of evaluating evidence and approving a direction — as opposed to the record of that act, which is an ADR.

## Why It Matters

Distinguishing the decision (an act of governance) from its record (a persistent artifact) matters because it clarifies where authority actually originates: acceptance creates architectural authority, and publishing an ADR only records that authority afterward.
