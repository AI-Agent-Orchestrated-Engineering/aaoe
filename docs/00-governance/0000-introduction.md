# Purpose

This document introduces Governance within AAOE.

Governance defines how engineering knowledge is created, validated,
organized, versioned, and evolved. Governance preserves the integrity of
the AAOE Body of Knowledge.

Governance governs engineering knowledge. It does not govern software
projects, and it does not prescribe engineering execution. Those concerns
belong to the [Operating Model](../03-operating-model/README.md).

---

# Why Governance Exists

Engineering knowledge accumulates over time. Without governance:

- terminology diverges
- observations become opinions
- specifications conflict
- evidence becomes disconnected
- historical decisions become difficult to understand
- engineering knowledge loses traceability

Governance provides the structure required for engineering knowledge to
evolve while preserving its history and integrity.

---

# Governance Scope

Governance applies to the evolution of:

- Discoveries
- Specifications
- Architectural Decision Records (ADRs)
- Case Studies
- Reference Architectures
- Knowledge Domains
- Terminology
- Versioning
- Evidence

Governance does not prescribe:

- implementation
- programming languages
- technology stacks
- cloud providers
- organizational structures
- software delivery processes

---

# Relationship to the Operating Model

The [Operating Model](../03-operating-model/README.md) defines how
engineering work is performed. Governance defines how engineering
knowledge evolves.

The Operating Model executes engineering. Governance preserves and evolves
engineering knowledge. These concerns are complementary.

---

# Relationship to Discoveries

[Discoveries](../02-discoveries/README.md) capture observed engineering
knowledge. Governance defines how Discoveries become part of the AAOE
Body of Knowledge.

---

# Relationship to Specifications

[Specifications](../01-standards/README.md) generalize validated
engineering knowledge. Governance defines how Specifications evolve while
preserving their historical context.

---

# Relationship to Case Studies

[Case Studies](../05-case-studies/README.md) provide the evidence from
which Discoveries emerge. Governance ensures that evidence remains
traceable throughout the evolution of engineering knowledge.

---

# Governance Principles

AAOE Governance is founded on the following principles:

- Evidence precedes guidance.
- Knowledge evolves through validation.
- History is preserved.
- Discoveries remain immutable.
- Specifications evolve transparently.
- Terminology remains consistent.
- Governance is explicit.
- Traceability is maintained.
