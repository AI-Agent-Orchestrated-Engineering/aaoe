# Knowledge Domains

## Specification Metadata

**Specification ID:** GV-0009

**Domain:** Governance

**Version:** 1.0

**Status:** Draft

---

# Purpose

This specification defines how engineering knowledge is organized into
Domains within the AAOE Body of Knowledge. Knowledge Domains improve
discoverability, governance, maintainability, and long-term evolution.

---

# Domain Principles

Knowledge Domains shall be:

- cohesive
- independently evolvable
- technology agnostic
- traceable
- discoverable
- minimally coupled
- governance compliant

Domains organize knowledge. Domains do not establish engineering
authority.

---

# Domain Responsibilities

Each Domain is responsible for governing a coherent area of engineering
knowledge.

Domains should:

- define their scope
- establish terminology
- maintain internal consistency
- preserve traceability
- evolve independently

---

# Domain Relationships

Domains may reference one another. Cross-domain references should remain
explicit and traceable. Dependencies between domains should be minimized.
Circular dependencies should be avoided whenever practical.

---

# Core Domains

The initial AAOE Body of Knowledge consists of:

- Governance
- Operating Model
- Discoveries
- Standards
- Reference Architectures
- Case Studies

Additional Domains may be introduced as AAOE evolves.

---

# Domain Evolution

Domains may:

- expand
- split
- merge
- become deprecated

Historical domain structures shall remain documented.

---

# Domain Identification

Every Domain shall have:

- unique identifier
- descriptive name
- documented purpose
- defined scope

Domain identifiers remain stable over time.

---

# Cross-Domain Traceability

Traceability may exist across Domains. Examples include:

- Discoveries influencing Specifications
- Specifications informing the Operating Model
- Case Studies producing Discoveries
- ADRs affecting Reference Architectures

Cross-domain relationships shall remain explicit.

---

# Governance

Creation of new Domains should demonstrate:

- a distinct body of knowledge
- clear scope
- minimal overlap
- governance value
- long-term maintainability

Domains should not be created solely for organizational convenience.

---

# Future Evolution

Future Governance Specifications may define:

- Domain Ownership
- Publication Governance
- Domain Maturity Models
